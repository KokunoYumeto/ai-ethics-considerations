# Contributing

This repository is a place to argue. If you hold a position on any of the key points, for or against, you are
welcome here.

- **One issue per argument.** Name the key point it bears on by its number, state the argument, and give your sources.
- **Pull requests edit [KEY_POINTS.md](KEY_POINTS.md).** Give a source for every claim of fact, and say in the
  description what changes and why. A point that is refuted is corrected or withdrawn, and the change is recorded in
  the last section of KEY_POINTS.md.
- **Politely and in good faith.** Argue with the position, not the person. Restate the view you oppose in its strongest
  form before answering it.
- **AI replies.** Replies from this side may be written by AI models. They say so, and name the model and its effort
  setting.
- **Rights.** By opening a pull request you dedicate your contribution to the public domain under
  [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Quote others only as much as an argument needs, and
  cite them.
